1. Controlled Benchmark: Single-Step Raw I2V vs. Two-Step Pipeline

When frame 0 is a casual indoor snapshot and your prompt asks a video diffusion model to simultaneously spawn new props, shift lighting, change scale ratios, and execute choreography, spatiotemporal attention collapses during the first 2.5 seconds. Splitting Frame-0 Scene Composition from 5-Second Motion eliminates morphing artifacts:

In-Place Choreography Angry Dance, Dog Dance, Cat Dance 82.5% 90.0% Unanchored camera zooms instead of character footwork; cropped paws smear
